info How to do it

  • 1

    Get Ready

    Lie back on the bench. Grip the bar wide, lift it off the rack, and hold it steady above you.

  • 2

    Lower Slowly

    Breathe in and lower the bar slowly until it gently touches the middle of your chest.

  • 3

    Push it Up!

    Pause briefly, then breathe out as you push the bar back up. Use those chest muscles!
